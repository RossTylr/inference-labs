## Epistemic checks

- Accuracy quoted for the wrong cohort or setting: HOLDS (cells 2 to 9, 12 to 16, 32 to 34; bayesian.py lines 73 to 82; app lines 58 to 61). Each scan figure is used in its own setting: E-16 before hospital, E-14 in hospital. The developer register at cell 2 speaks of drawing accuracy from a published two-by-two table; cell 13 says E-14 has none and that the in-hospital Beta shapes are an assumption. The one cross-setting use is the failure case, where it is the point and is named as such. The statements that the 25% describes a scan called positive and that the paper does not make clear how indeterminate scans enter its table are the register's own (partyka2022 brief). E-31 supplies only the 90 mm Hg threshold; its accuracy is not quoted. Cell 32 calls the borrowed accuracy "the commonest version" of the failure; that is a judgement, not a measured frequency, and may be claimed only as one.
- Proxy data read as the real thing: HOLDS (cells 16 to 20, 27, 28, 33 to 37, 41 to 43; app lines 25 to 26 and 58 to 61). Every result on the generator is called a mechanism, and the CRASH-3 fixture is called synthetic, random and clinically meaningless where it is used. The app's closing caption exempts only the trial's absolute reduction, which is a published figure given with its id. Two limits on what may be claimed. Scorecard row 8 is 0.998 because the CRASH-2 prior on its own gives 0.997; the fixture's interval runs from 0.41 to 1.54, so the row measures the prior, not the fixture, and says nothing about tranexamic acid. The pre-hospital belief in cell 34 meets the generator's count because the generator draws its scans with E-16's accuracy (generator.py lines 278 to 283), so that agreement holds by construction.
- Civilian data read as military: HOLDS (cells 5, 21, 25, 29; bayesian.py lines 25 to 27, 84 to 85 and 473 to 482, shown at app line 33; app line 60). The scan studies are called civilian. Cell 5 adds that no military figure for the scan was found and that using the studies for a military casualty is an assumption; the register holds no military scan figure (E-14 to E-17). The relative risk of bleeding death by delay is now named as CRASH-2's wherever it is applied to the casualty: cell 29, the library's docstring and comment, and the app's treatment caption. Each place states that using a civilian trial's figures for a military casualty is an assumption. PROPPR, STAAMP, the brain injury trial, CRASH-2, CRASH-3 and RePHILL are nowhere described as military, and RePHILL is placed in English prehospital critical care services, as E-77 places it. The ABC score supplies only a threshold.
- A diagnosis result read as a treatment result: HOLDS (cells 29 to 31, 41, 42; bayesian.py lines 267 to 271, 388 to 390 and 466 to 496, shown at app lines 31 to 33 and 55). The app now labels the product "Belief times relative reduction (mechanism)". The caption directly beneath the metrics describes it as the belief multiplied by CRASH-2's relative reduction for the delay. It calls this a modelling step, a diagnosis result put to use and not a treatment result, and not a change in this casualty's risk of death. The findings line repeats the name and calls the number a mechanism figure. The caption is right: a change in the casualty's risk would also need her risk of death if bleeding (design section 12.4), which no step supplies. The lens's `treat` returns the belief unchanged, and the scorecard keeps its one treatment row apart. In the notebook, cell 31 says the same product is a diagnosis result put to use, not a treatment result. One limit: cell 31 calls the range 0.11 to 0.31 "the plausible benefit", and the notebook does not give the absolute scale the app now gives. The 0.21 may be claimed only as belief times relative reduction, not as a benefit to the casualty in any unit.

## Numbers and their evidence ids

- Scan accuracy: HOLDS (cells 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 13; app line 59). 21 of 84, 257 of 266, 25% and 96%, the third of abdominal scans indeterminate, the physician-staffed civilian helicopter service and the blunt trauma are in E-16 and the partyka2022 brief; 74% and 98% are in E-14. Beta(74, 26) and Beta(294, 6) are marked as an assumption. The statement that both studies are civilian (cell 5, app line 60) goes beyond the netherton2019 brief, which says only that its pooled studies are largely in hospital; it narrows the claim rather than widening it.
- Counts and grids: HOLDS (cell 4). 108 patients, 32% and 55% are in E-64; the frequency finding is E-114, used without its number.
- PROPPR and its reanalysis: HOLDS (cell 21). P=.12 is E-72; 93% is E-73.
- CRASH-2: HOLDS (cells 21, 24). 0.91 (0.85 to 0.97) and the prespecified rule on subgroups are in E-20. Cell 24 calls CRASH-2's patients "bleeding into the trunk"; the register describes them as trauma patients with significant haemorrhage, wherever the bleeding. The sentence's point, that exchangeability with head injury is a clinical question, does not depend on it.
- CRASH-3: HOLDS (cells 21, 22, 24). 0.94 (0.86 to 1.02) is E-27's primary result. Cells 22 and 24 name it as the result for the 9,202 treated within 3 hours. Cell 21 reports it as what CRASH-3 found, without attributing it to all 12,737 randomised. 0.78 (0.64 to 0.95) is E-28.
- STAAMP, the brain injury trial and the missing reanalysis: HOLDS (cell 21). E-74 is used without a number. The statement that its certainty came from the earlier trial follows from E-74's posterior interval being CRASH-2's own (0.85 to 0.97, E-20). 0.1 and 0.025 are in E-79. E-33 supplies no number. E-108 is used without a number, so its open item touches nothing here.
- RePHILL: HOLDS (cells 25 to 27). Every figure is in E-77, and the probabilities are marked as calculated here, not reported by the trial, as the background file requires.
- Tranexamic acid by delay: HOLDS (cells 29, 30; bayesian.py lines 84 to 93 and 473 to 482; app lines 31 to 33). All three bands match E-22, and the log-normal shape is stated as an assumption. The new figures are in E-22: within an hour, 7.7% of the placebo group and 5.3% of the tranexamic acid group died from bleeding, and 2.4 points is the difference. Three limits on what may be claimed:
  - E-23 records that the timing analysis is exploratory, and neither cell 29's "reduced death from bleeding" nor the app's "found" says so. The figures by delay may be claimed only as an exploratory subgroup result.
  - The app's scale figure is always the first hour's. Yet 386 of the 868 casualties in each setting fall in the 1 to 3 hour band, where E-22's difference is 1.3 points (6.1% against 4.8%). The metric names its band, so it is not misattributed, but it is the scale for the first hour only.
  - Eight casualties in each setting fall in the band over 3 hours, where E-22 records more deaths from bleeding with tranexamic acid (4.4% against 3.1%, RR 1.44). For them the product is negative; for haemorrhage casualty 90 before hospital it is -0.04 (-0.07 to -0.01). The caption's "relative reduction ... that CRASH-2 found" must then be read as a signed quantity, and the page shows that late treatment was associated with more deaths only through the negative product.

  The band is set by the time to pre-hospital care (bayesian.py line 517), which is when the generator gives tranexamic acid (generator.py line 288), and the findings line says so.
- Triage threshold: HOLDS (cell 16; bayesian.py line 64). 90 mm Hg is in E-31.
- Predictive coding: HOLDS (cell 44). E-55, and the cell stays within what the rao1999 brief allows.
- Generator figures: HOLDS (cells 17, 19, 33, 35, 36, 42). Each is counted on the generator and labelled as such. Cell 35's two structural statements were checked against the generator.
