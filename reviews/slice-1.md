## Limits

- Cell 5 does not say that E-16's service was physician-staffed, nor that fewer than half of those scanned had hospital data for comparison, the partyka2022 brief's reasons why E-16 cannot support military pre-hospital use; the 25% and 96% may be claimed only as accuracy measured with physicians scanning, carried to a medic by assumption.
- E-15 gives free-fluid specificity of 95% in hypotensive patients against 98% in normotensive adults; the lens updates on a pressure of 90 or less and then on the scan with the pooled 98%, so in hospital a belief after a positive scan at or below 90 mm Hg holds only on the generator, where the two are drawn independently.
- The lens's pre-hospital specificity is Beta(257, 9), mean 96.6%, from E-16's counts, while the generator draws pre-hospital scans at E-16's published 96% (`params.yaml`, `scan_accuracy_pre_hospital`); over seeds 42 to 61 the belief in cell 33 exceeds the count by 4.3 points on average (2.2 to 6.7, on every seed, seed 42 the closest), and cell 20's group at 0.476 runs 3.0 points high on average, so the agreement in cells 20 and 34 is close, not exact, and not by construction as the twelfth review said.
- Cell 45's lead for the lens in hospital holds partly by construction: the generator draws the scan with the accuracy the lens assumes and against the target the scan looks for, while the rule reads no scan and was built for another target; the same applies to cell 44's 4.3 and 9.3 against 3.1.
- Cells 44 and 45 report no spread across seeds (lesson 12); over seeds 42 to 61 none of the comparisons they draw is a tie: the rule's sensitivity 58.1% (standard deviation 1.2) against the lens's 5.0% (0.5) before hospital, the lens's 74.0% (1.3) and 98.1% (0.2) against the rule's 58.1% and 57.4% in hospital, and the lens's net benefit above the rule's by 0.99 per 100 (0.16) before hospital and 6.13 (0.12) in hospital.
- Cell 44's MPTT-24 row, 3.13 against 3.02 for treating all, is the closest of 20 seeds; over seeds 42 to 61 the rule is above treating all on every seed by 0.11 to 0.45 per 100, mean 0.30, against a target it was not written for, and the row may not be read as its decision value on its own target.
- Cell 44's rows are on the 29,925 battle casualties of the 100,000 sample at seed 42 with the prior rounded to 0.12, and cell 42's on the 8,909 of the 30,000 sample with the unrounded 12.3%; the axis 7 rows and the rows above them may not be read as one sample.
- The counts behind the prior and the triage reading, and the scorecard, include battle casualties whom the generator has dead from bleeding before pre-hospital care (86 of 5,882, 156 of 8,909, 482 of 29,925), and the walk-through offers 10 such casualties before hospital and 28 dead before Role 2 in hospital; among those alive at pre-hospital care the share bleeding into the abdomen is 11.4%, not 12.3%, and no statement changes direction, but the figures may not be read as figures for casualties who reach care.
- Cell 16's "it can be counted" holds within the generator; the 12.3% is set by E-35's mechanism counts, three assumed parameters and the assumption that free fluid arises from truncal bleeding only, and may be claimed as counted, not as known.
- The baseline's Brier score and calibration row, 0.422 and 0.314, describe the generator's mix, 12.8% bleeding into the abdomen and 44.2% in Priority 1, not the rule.
- Scorecard row 8, 0.998, measures the CRASH-2 prior, which alone gives 0.997, and not the fixture, whose interval runs from 0.41 to 1.54; it says nothing about tranexamic acid although its metric name reads as if it did.
- The treatment caption's "the belief that this casualty is bleeding" is the belief that she is bleeding into the abdomen; the product applies a reduction in death from bleeding at any site to that narrower belief.
- For the 8 casualties of each setting beyond 3 hours the product is negative, -0.04 before hospital and -0.01 in hospital, and the findings line calls it harm; that is the model's reading of E-22's exploratory result, not a measured harm to this casualty.
- E-23 records the timing analysis as exploratory, and neither cell 29's "reduced death from bleeding" nor the panel's "found" says so; the figures by delay may be claimed only as an exploratory subgroup result.
- The panel's scale figure is always the first hour's 2.4 points; 386 of the 868 casualties of each setting fall in the 1 to 3 hour band, where E-22's difference is 1.3 points, and 8 in the band over 3 hours, where it runs the other way, so for them the product has no absolute figure of its own beside it (lesson 3), though the metric names its band.
- Cell 44's "net benefit" is not defined in this notebook and its method's source, E-93, is not on its evidence list; it is not a count of casualties who gain from any treatment.
- Cell 25's "showed no difference: 64.3% against 64.8%" may be claimed only as no difference observed; E-77's interval allows nine points either way.
- Cell 27's 92% is the probability on a flat prior that the relative risk of death within 3 hours is below one; it is about the effect, not the count of deaths.
- In hospital the decision panel acts on the belief after triage while the band's Evacuation point is the belief after the in-hospital scan, 10.2% against 3% for haemorrhage casualty 1, so the band's evacuation belief may not be read as the one the decision panel acts on.
- The figure titles at cells 15, 20, 23, 37 and 39 add a reading to what is plotted ("barely moves it", "the lens is calibrated", "no prior makes a large benefit likely", "independent findings combine soundly", "nothing warns you"); each is borne out by the plot, but lesson 6 asks for titles that describe what is plotted.
- The panel file's docstring says every number comes from `bayesian.page_panel`; the dotted line is drawn at `bayesian.EVACUATE_ABOVE`, a library constant that `page_panel` does not return.
