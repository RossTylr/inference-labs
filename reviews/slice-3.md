- The civilian cohort as cell 12 describes it: HOLDS (cell 12, second paragraph). The paragraph reads, after the figures: "In the generator the civilian heart rate is assumed 6 beats a minute lower, and civilian heart and respiratory rates are assumed to rise less with bleeding and tension pneumothorax. One limit:
  - "It lowers sensitivity alone" means it lowers sensitivity and not specificity; it may not be read as the only cause of the fall in sensitivity. What may be claimed is that the smaller response accounts for most of the fall in sensitivity and none of the gain in specificity, and the lower heart rate for most of the rest of the fall and nearly all of the gain.
