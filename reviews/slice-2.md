The score has a second published operating point, E-90's 0.36 and 0.80, which cell 38 gives and the panel does not. 3. Axis 2's "flags 11.2 points more of the casualties than are in need", and the Brier score beside it, follow from the generator's mix, in which 16.7% of the 100,000 need an intervention and 27.9% are put in Priority 1.
